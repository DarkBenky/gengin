## Confirmed Wins
- PR #234 - render/color/color.h: UnpackColor becomes static inline at its call sites. Frame avg -1.0% / median -1.2%, image_mse 0.00, all frame hashes identical.
- PR #235 - simulation/cSim/flightControl.c: the extended-miss arc drops samples as the target gets faster. Pinned flight suite miss -7.4%, hit rate 0.600 -> 0.700, no tier regression.

## Failed Approaches
- skybox/skybox.c sampler, face selection and clamp math (flame 8.0% excl): stubbing the sampler to a constant is -5.1% avg / -5.0% median of the frame, but keeping the index math and dropping only the texel load is the same -5.2%/-5.3%, so the entire cost is the DRAM read and the branches are free. Micro-bench (96 MB, 921600 dirs, 32 threads): 0.33 ns/call coherent vs 1.45 ns/call scattered; the frame's 184.6k samples/frame are the coherent case and already sit on the latency roofline. MADV_HUGEPAGE on the 96 MB: scattered 29.23 -> 27.41 ns 1-thread, 1.45 -> 1.52 ns 32-thread (no gain). Footprint reduction was already refuted by #121 (half-resolution cube, +3.4%). A face-select LUT over the direction is both slower (6.40 vs 3.34 ns/call coherent) and not exact (653 of 4M texels differ: fast-math reassociation moves the texel index at face boundaries).
- render/cpu/ray.c glare pass depth-span pruning (RayTraceLightRowFunc): skipping the (tile,row) pairs whose depth span holds no shaded pixel is exactly equivalent (image_mse 0.00, all 10 frame hashes identical) and the racy diagnostic counters reported roughly two thirds of the pairs skipped, yet the pinned bench measures avg -0.1% / median +0.1% / p99 -1.0%, i.e. inside noise, and an earlier single 3 s run that suggested -1.7% did not reproduce. Either the pass is far cheaper in wall time than its 7.6% flame share suggests, or the skipped pairs are the cheap ones - either way a prune of that pass does not clear the ~1% noise floor.
- flightControl.c effortWeight 0, seed-only command, analytic roll channel, range-scheduled command damping, TARGET_LEAD_GAIN sweep, acceleration-hedged lead, extended-term-only full lead: see the flight node map for the numbers. Everything that loosens the command damping or raises the lead improves the aggregate miss by up to 13% but breaks one tier (static collapses without the damping, step loses its hairline hit with any lead above 0.3). The only change that helped nothing but cost nothing was the lead in the extended term only (+4.7% controller cost for -9.1% miss).
- flightControl.c lead and extended-term knobs measured on 2026-11-21: the aggregate wants more lead (full lead: 58.027, drift -87.5%, jink -13.3%, 19 of 20 scenarios better) but step:3 is a knife-edge that flips at any lead above 0.3, and the extended term's horizon/weight are chaotic because the static tier (1.105 m) re-draws by tens of percent from codegen-only differences. What survived: keep every constant, and drop arc *samples* (not their spacing) in proportion to target speed - that leaves the stationary case bit-identical, which is what the tier gate punishes.
